## Product drawings
The three products are original cel drawings. Their proportions, colours, label layout and vertical wordmark were measured from the brand's packshots on the pages above. The photos were used as reference only; no photo appears in the film.

## The character
The girl is an original cartoon character. The braids and freckles nod to the brand's campaign styling, but she is not a likeness of any real person.

## Everything else
Every drawing, texture, animation, music cue and sound effect was generated procedurally for this film (code in this folder). There are no third-party samples, stock footage or stock illustrations.
